Haushaltsbuch Barrierefrei is an intuitive and privacy-focused personal finance manager designed specifically for blind, visually impaired, and accessibility-conscious users.

Features:
- Optimized for screen readers (TalkBack, accessible live-regions and shortcuts)
- High-contrast themes, scalable typography, and OLED dark mode
- Flexible budget periods: Monthly, weekly, or custom intervals
- Savings pots, recurring transactions, categories, and wishlists
- Local encrypted vault for 100% offline data privacy (zero tracking)
- Optional PIN and biometric security
- Easy data backup and export
